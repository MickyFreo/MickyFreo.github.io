▶ HarpMaya Tube

A private, family-only YouTube viewer for our Fire TV and Android TV.

What it does

  • Searches YouTube using the official YouTube Data API.
  • Only shows videos above a minimum length chosen by a parent, so short clips don't appear.
  • Lets a parent set a daily watching limit for each family member, protected by a passcode.
  • Plays videos in YouTube's official embedded player.
  • Optionally lets a family member sign in with Google to see their own subscriptions, liked videos and playlists (read-only).

Who it's for

HarpMaya Tube is a personal project used only by one family on their own TVs. It is not sold, advertised or distributed to the public.

Privacy policy

HarpMaya Tube uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
