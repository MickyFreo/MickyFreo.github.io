Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

HarpMaya Tube is a personal app used by one family on their own TVs. This policy explains what information it uses and where that information stays.

Information the app uses

  • Search terms. When you search, your search words are sent to YouTube (Google) to get results. The app does not save your searches.
  • Google account (optional). If a family member chooses to sign in, the app asks Google for read-only access to their YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It uses this only to show that person's own subscriptions, liked videos, playlists and channel name on the TV. The app cannot upload, post, comment, subscribe, like or delete anything.
  • Settings on the TV. The parent passcode (stored as a one-way hash), video-length settings, daily watching limits and today's watching time are stored only on the TV itself.

Where information is stored

Everything the app keeps (sign-in tokens, settings and watching time) stays in the app's private storage on the TV. Nothing is sent to the app's developer, and there is no developer server, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Videos play in YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode. The app clears the player's cookies and stored web data before and after each video.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer anyone's information. Information is exchanged only with Google/YouTube as needed to search and play videos and, for signed-in users, to read their own YouTube lists.

Google and YouTube

HarpMaya Tube uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

HarpMaya Tube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In the app, choose the user button, then Sign out of …. This deletes that person's sign-in from the TV and asks Google to cancel the app's access.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the TV.

Children

The app is set up and supervised by a parent, who controls video length, daily limits and which profiles need a passcode.
